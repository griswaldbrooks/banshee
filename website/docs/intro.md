# Banshee

This is the project hub for **Banshee**, a combat robot fielded annually at [Weaponized Plastic](https://weaponizedplastic.com/).

The repo lives at [github.com/griswaldbrooks/banshee](https://github.com/griswaldbrooks/banshee).

## What's here

- [Bot overview](banshee.md) — what Banshee is.
- [Build & Flash](build-flash.md) — getting the firmware onto the bot.
- [Electronics](electronics.md) — wiring, pinout, power.
- [Mechanicals](mechanicals.md) — chassis, drivetrain, weapon.
- [BOM](bom.md) — bill of materials and spares.
- [Onshape](onshape.md) — CAD links.
- [Seasons](seasons/) — per-event logs.
- [Links](links.md) — everything else.
